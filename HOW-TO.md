# How to update the NeatBliss site

Hi. This is for you, not for a developer. If you can follow a recipe, you can do everything here.

Everything you can change about the site lives in one file, and there is a private page that helps you write it. You never have to touch code.

## The private settings page

Your settings page is here:

`/7ae5fff6-e9af-4876-86b8-8dfb7a1a0811`

Add that to the end of your site address in your browser. For example, if the site is at neatblisscleaning.com, the settings page is neatblisscleaning.com/7ae5fff6-e9af-4876-86b8-8dfb7a1a0811.

Bookmark that page in your browser now, so you can find it any time. It is private. It is not linked from anywhere on the site. Nobody who visits the site will stumble onto it.

## Change the message at the top of the site

Use this when your schedule is full, or when you have news everyone should see before they call.

1. Open your bookmark for the settings page.
2. Find the card called **Message at the top of the site**.
3. Flip the toggle to **Showing**.
4. Type your message in the MESSAGE box. Keep it short and warm.
5. Click **Preview my changes**. The site opens with your message at the top so you can see how it looks. If it needs a tweak, click **Back to editing** in the little pill at the bottom of the screen.
6. Back on the settings page, click **Copy my settings**. The button says **Copied!** for a moment. Your settings are now on your clipboard.
7. Click **Open the settings file on GitHub**. A new tab opens with the current settings file.
8. On the GitHub page, click the little pencil icon to edit. Select everything in the file, then paste. The old settings are replaced by the new ones you just copied.
9. Scroll to the bottom of the GitHub page. Type a short note in the message box if you like, then click the green **Commit changes** button. Confirm on the next screen.
10. Wait about a minute. Then open your site in a new tab. Your message is up.

To take the message down: open the settings page, flip the toggle to **Hidden**, then follow steps 6 through 10.

## If GitHub sends you an email that says something failed

That email is normal, and it means the site did not update. Nothing broke. Visitors still see the site exactly as it was before you tried.

This usually happens because a copy and paste missed a piece of the file. The fix is the same steps as before:

1. Open your bookmark for the settings page.
2. Make sure your changes are still in the form. If not, redo them.
3. Click **Copy my settings** again.
4. Click **Open the settings file on GitHub**. Click the pencil. Select all. Paste.
5. Scroll down. Click the green **Commit changes** button. Confirm.
6. Wait about a minute and check the site.

If you get the failure email a second time, take a screenshot of it and send it along with what you were trying to change. Then leave the site alone. It is still fine.

## Change your phone number, services, or reviews

It is the exact same flow as the message. The settings page has a card for each thing:

- **Business details** for the business name, tagline, phone for calls, phone for texts, email, Facebook page link, and service area.
- **Services** for the three service cards. You can add a service with the **+ Add a service** button. You can remove one with the trash icon. There must always be at least one service.
- **Testimonials** for the reviews you copy over from Facebook. Copy the words exactly. Add or remove reviews the same way as services.

For any of them:

1. Open the settings page.
2. Edit the card you want to change.
3. Click **Preview my changes** if you want to see it first. Click **Back to editing** when you are done looking.
4. Click **Copy my settings**.
5. Click **Open the settings file on GitHub**, pencil, select all, paste.
6. Click the green **Commit changes** button.
7. Wait about a minute. Refresh the site.

## Two small things worth knowing

- Nothing you type on the settings page changes the real site until you finish the GitHub steps. You can play with the form as much as you want and nobody sees anything.
- **Preview my changes** is only for you, only on this browser, only on this device. It is a safe way to look before you send.

That is it. If you can do this once, you can do it every time.
